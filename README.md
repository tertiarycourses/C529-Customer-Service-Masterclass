## About the course

This course equips participants with the skills to assess and address customer needs by collecting and analysing customer feedback. Learners develop an understanding of customer expectations, explore communication channels such as face-to-face, phone and email, and identify opportunities to improve service quality. Through realistic Singapore scenarios, participants strengthen their ability to deliver consistently good service.

Learners also practise managing difficult customers and deciding when to escalate, and learn how to generate return business by closing the loop on feedback and acting on operational insight.

## Learning outcomes

| | Outcome |
|---|---|
| **LO1** | Collect and analyse customer feedback to assess needs and expectations using effective communication principles. |
| **LO2** | Identify and recommend areas for improvement based on customer feedback and operational insights. |
